BEHIND THE WORDS

MEET THE AUTHOR.

Edward Kress is an independent horror author, publisher, and storyteller from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Through Steel City Publishing he creates dark fiction spanning extreme horror, splatterpunk, supernatural terror, psychological suspense, cryptid encounters, and cosmic dread.

His fiction ranges from standalone novels to multi-book sagas and interconnected horror worlds. The industrial neighborhoods, forgotten spaces, and Appalachian wilderness of Western Pennsylvania often inspire his stories.

Kress also develops short-form horror storytelling through 60 Second Horror Stories.
